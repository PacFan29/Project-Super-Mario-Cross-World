I recommend playing with a gamepad.

<Common Actions>
L-Stick: Move
A: Jump
Hold RT or X + L-Stick: Dash (Team Mega Man: Hold RT only. Excludes Team Sonic.)
RB: Crouch
RB + L-Stick: Move while crouching
RB in mid-air: Ground Pound (Excludes Team Pac-Man and Team Sonic).
X: Fireball, etc. (Excludes Team Mega Man.)
Press X near an object: Pick up object
Hold object and release X: Throw object
Hold object, hold RB, and release X: Place object down
Hold object, hold LB, and release X: Throw object upwards


<Team Mario>
Hold RB + A: Crouch jump
RB + A while moving: Long jump
RB + X: Roll
A while rolling: Rolling long jump
RB + X in mid-air: Somersault (Body attack)
Hold L-Stick in opposite direction + A: Side somersault
A while touching a wall: Wall kick
A after Ground Pound: Ground Pound jump

・Princess Peach
Hold A in mid-air: Float jump

・Yoshi
Hold A in mid-air: Flutter jump
X: Eat enemy
Y: Throw egg
RB while aiming egg: Cancel egg throw

・Rosalina
X: Spin (Double jump in mid-air)


<Team Pac-Man>
A in mid-air or (LB or RB) in mid-air: Butt bounce
Hold Y: Pac-Dash
Y in mid-air: Flip kick

Hidden Skill: Fruit Target
LT: Fruit Target
LT or X while summoned: Throw
LB or RB while summoned: Hold


<Team Mega Man>
X: Weapon attack
RB + A: Slide

<Team Sonic>

RB while moving: Roll
RB + Rapidly press A: Spin Dash
RT: Spin Dash (hold to charge)
RB in mid-air: Stomp

・Classic Sonic
Hold A in mid-air: Drop Dash

・Tails
Hold A in mid-air: Propeller Flight
Y: Tail Spin Attack

・Amy
A in mid-air: Double Jump
Hold A after double jump to land: Hammer Rush Attack
Y: Hammer Attack
RB + Y: Hammer Jump

・Knuckles
Hold A in mid-air: Glide
Y: Punch

・Modern Sonic
(A or X) while jumping: Jump Dash / Homing Attack

LT: Drift
