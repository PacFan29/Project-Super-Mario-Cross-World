Superball Flower, from Super Mario Land

Model and all textures made from scratch by Peardian using Cinema 4D and Fireworks.


Info:
I made this after studying the truly inspirational 3D artwork in Brawl.
The style is mostly based on regular Super Mario items, with a hint of Brawl's shading.
The roundness of the flower is slightly inspired by Super Mario Bros. Wonder.
The model has eyes to look modern, but you can remove them to look more authentic.
There are four different color sets for the flower and ball.
The first color uses sepia petals and green leaves. The Superball is gray like in the official artwork.
The second color is blue, based on an image I found on Mariowiki. I don't know its origin. The Superball is green like in the manga.
The third color is sepiatoned, like in Super Mario Maker and Nintendo Switch Online.
The fourth color is green, like in the alternate Switch Online palette based on original Game Boys.
There are five different environment options, plus a specular highlight map, so choose the one you like best.
I included two mask textures that correspond to the petals and center of the flower, so that it can be made to glow like the sprites.
Unlike normal, I didn't aim for a particular scale with this one. Please resize as needed.
